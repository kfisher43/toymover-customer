2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information created in the course of a shipment, and limited information gathered automatically when you use the Site.

Information you provide

Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Identity and contact details: name, phone number, email address, and billing or mailing address
  • Pickup and delivery details: addresses, business or residence names, gate codes or pins, lot numbers, hours, and the names and phone numbers of people meeting the driver
  • Shipment and vehicle details: item type and description, year, make, model, dimensions, condition (including whether the item runs), vehicle identification numbers (VINs), serial numbers, auction or lot numbers, keys or “no keys” notes, and any special handling instructions
  • Documents and media: photographs, certificates of origin, bills of lading, condition reports, authorizations, invoices, and other documents you send us or that are created for the job
  • Payment information: payment method, the status of an invoice, and related billing notes. We do not encourage you to send full payment-card numbers by email or text. If a third-party processor is used, their terms and privacy policy apply to the card data they handle.
  • Communications: the content of emails, phone calls, voicemails, website form messages, and text messages you exchange with us
  • SMS program choices: whether you opted in or out of Toymover shipment notifications, the number enrolled, and related consent records

You may also subscribe to email updates on the Site (for example, to hear about specials, sales, or events). That subscription is separate from SMS consent.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and standard website tools may collect:

  • Internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, device type, and general request logs
  • Pages visited, referring URL, and approximate dates and times of visits
  • Cookie and similar technology data, as described in the “Cookies” section below

The Site may also use security tools such as Google reCAPTCHA on contact forms, which can collect device and usage information according to Google’s own policies.

Information from others

We may receive information from a shipper, consignee, dealer, auction, storage facility, driver, or carrier involved in a job (for example, a pickup authorization, a gate pin, or a delivery contact). We treat that information as shipment data and use it to perform the job.

We do not knowingly buy marketing lists in order to send unsolicited promotional text messages.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to quote requests and questions, and prepare estimates
  • Book, schedule, dispatch, and complete shipments
  • Communicate with you and with pickup or delivery contacts about status, timing, access instructions, and documents
  • Issue and collect invoices, and maintain our business records
  • Prepare bills of lading, condition reports, and other shipping documents
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Site, including preventing spam and abuse
  • Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and protect the rights, safety, and property of Toymover, our drivers, customers, and the public
  • Send Toymover shipment notifications by text only if you have opted in, as described below and in our Terms of Service
  • Send optional email updates if you subscribed to them, until you unsubscribe

We do not use SMS opt-in data for promotional profiling, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Text messages and SMS consent

Toymover offers an optional text-messaging program called Toymover shipment notifications, typically sent from +1 (866) 569-8830. If you opt in, we may text you about quote follow-ups and pickup or delivery status updates, as described in the SMS program terms in our Terms of Service.

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of requesting a quote or using any Toymover service. You can work with us by phone or email without joining the SMS program. Any consent checkbox on a Toymover form is optional and should be left unchecked unless you want text updates.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to a Toymover text, or by emailing toymover@gmail.com or calling (972) 256-6760. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

That means we do not sell, rent, or share the fact that you opted in, or the mobile number you used to opt in, with anyone for their marketing or promotional use. We use that information only to administer the SMS program and to provide the shipment-related messages you requested.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to run the business and complete a job, or as described here:

  • Drivers and carriers: We share the pickup and delivery details, contact information, and vehicle or item information a driver or contracted carrier needs to perform the shipment. We do not share SMS opt-in records with drivers for marketing purposes.
  • Service providers: We use vendors that help us operate the Site, host email, process payments, provide communications (including our text-messaging provider, such as Twilio), keep records, or secure our forms. These providers may process information only to perform services for us, and we do not authorize them to use Toymover customer data for their own marketing.
  • Parties to the shipment: We may share status or document information with the shipper, consignee, dealer, auction, or other party who has a legitimate role in that job.
  • Legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if we believe it is required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: If we sell, merge, or reorganize the business, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy or a successor policy with at least the same level of protection for SMS opt-in data.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not allow third parties to use our SMS program to market to you.

For clarity, and consistent with the SMS commitment above: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to operate pages, remember a cookie-banner choice, analyze website traffic, and help us understand how the Site is used. Our cookie banner, when displayed, explains that we use cookies to analyze website traffic and optimize your website experience, and that by accepting, your data may be aggregated with other user data.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Site functions. Third-party tools (for example, analytics or reCAPTCHA) may set their own cookies under their own policies.

7. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide services, maintain shipment and accounting records, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and meet legal, tax, and insurance requirements.

Quote requests that do not become bookings may be kept for a shorter period, unless we need them for follow-up you requested or for our records. SMS consent and opt-out records are retained as needed to honor your choice and to demonstrate compliance with carrier and messaging rules.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or de-identify it in a reasonably practical manner, subject to backup and archival systems.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the size and nature of our business to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include limiting access to shipment and customer records to people who need them for their work, using password-protected systems, and transmitting texts and emails through reputable providers.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data (such as full payment-card numbers or driver’s license images) through unsecured email or text unless we specifically request a document needed for a job.
